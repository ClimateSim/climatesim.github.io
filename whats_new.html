A running changelog of new interactive charts, Met Office & peer-reviewed datasets, and usability improvements (starting from 16 November 2026). Tap any entry to jump straight to the updated page or section.

📅 26 November 2026
v7.4.0 – v7.9.3
  • 🤝 ANTIDOTE (antidotelive.studio) & Local Community Action (#local-community-action)
    Added ANTIDOTE's grassroots collective-action platform alongside WLCAN under a dedicated Local Community Action subheading with a direct #local-community-action anchor on What Can I Do?.
    Local Action →
  • 🧊 Arctic Sea Ice Topic on Impacts & Streamlined Menu
    Added Arctic Sea Ice Collapse & Ice–Albedo Feedback as a collapsible topic panel on the Impacts page (linking to the full interactive lab), moved Snow in Scotland & Arctic Sea Ice out of the top hamburger menu, and added What's New to the More Facts hamburger menu.
    Impacts Page →
  • ❄️ UK Air Frost Days Climate Stripes (1931–2025)
    Added official Met Office HadUK-Grid UK annual air frost days to Climate Stripes—showing frost days falling 26% from 64.5 days/yr (1961–1990) to 47.5 days/yr (2016–2025), complete with 5-year spline and 30-year trend lines.
    Open Stripes →
  • 🔥 Central England Days ≥ 30°C Climate Stripes (1878–2025)
    Added official Met Office HadCET daily maximum counts for days reaching 30.0°C or above—showing frequency jumping 6.3× from 0.27 days/yr (1878–1990) to 1.70 days/yr (2016–2025), peaking at 7 days in 2022 and 9 in 1976.
    Open Stripes →
  • ⏳ 1,000,000-Year Time Span Slider & Paleoclimate Peak Badges
    Added quick-preset buttons (500y to 1My) and a 500-year increment slider below the main warming chart, seamless orange paleoclimate curves, clickable interglacial peak badges showing Peak T, preceding 100-year ΔT, and peer-reviewed DOI links, plus matching ΔT₁₀₀y on the 2026 badge and mobile-scrollable popups across the app.
    Open Simulator →
